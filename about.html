Our Story

Where it all started

Micky Tech Solutions was founded with a clear conviction: every Kenyan business deserves a powerful online presence, regardless of size or budget. We started small — just a laptop and a vision — building websites for local entrepreneurs who had no digital footprint.

Over time, we grew into a full technology studio offering web development, custom software systems, UI/UX design and maintenance services. Today we work with clients across Kenya and beyond, delivering solutions that are fast, secure and built to last.

Our edge isn't just technical skill — it's the relationship we build with every client. We listen, we advise, and we deliver work we're genuinely proud of.
